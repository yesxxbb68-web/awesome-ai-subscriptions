## How to read this

- List prices are official USD figures, cited below, excluding tax. Checkout may add tax or show a local-currency price.
- Most vendors say usage is not a fixed message count. This guide only repeats multipliers the vendors themselves publish (for example, Claude Max = 5× Pro).
- Model names change. Prefer [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) and the latest help articles; use the in-product model picker for what your account actually has.
- This is about consumer subscriptions, not API token pricing, except where a plan includes a stated monthly API credit.

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Official comparison: [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/). Personal plans: **Free, Go, Plus, Pro**. Go, Plus, and Pro are billed monthly. Official help articles say **annual billing and paying several months in advance are not available**.

| Plan | Official price (USD / month) | Official positioning | Best for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Free access | Occasional use | [Pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) |
| Go | **$8** in the US; some markets have localized prices | Higher tool, upload, image, and memory limits than Free | Everyday chat and light work when Free image/upload caps get in the way | [Introducing ChatGPT Go](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/) (2026-01-16); [What is ChatGPT Go?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11989085-what-is-chatgpt-go) |
| Plus | **$20** | Advanced work and productivity | Stronger reasoning, Deep Research, more Codex / ChatGPT Work, earlier features | [What is ChatGPT Plus?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus) |
| Pro 100 | **$100** | One of three Pro tiers; no Ultrafast | Heavy use beyond Plus | [About ChatGPT Pro tiers](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) |
| Pro 200 | **$200** | More usage than Pro 100 | Frequent research and coding, longer Codex / Work sessions | Same article |
| Pro 500 | **$500** | Highest included usage; includes Ultrafast | Users who need Ultrafast on GPT-6 Astra / GPT-6.1 Sol | Same article |

The pricing page lists Plus as including GPT-6 advanced reasoning, expanded messages and uploads, richer image creation, expanded deep research, memory and context, Projects / scheduled tasks / GPTs, expanded Codex and ChatGPT Work, and early features. Pro adds tiered usage, GPT-6 Astra pro reasoning, longer sessions, the Dot agent, and faster / higher image limits. Check the live page for the current checklist.

Other official notes, verified 2026-11-15:

- API usage is **not** included in Go or Plus.
- Everyday text chat is unlimited on Free and Go; image generation, uploads, and data analysis still have limits and abuse guardrails.
- Any “unlimited” Pro access is still subject to abuse guardrails. Eligible features can continue on purchased credits after the included allowance; you cannot bypass a limit in settings.
- Ultrafast is available **only on Pro 500** among Pro plans.
- Web checkout accepts major credit cards. App Store / Google Play subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google and follow those stores’ rules.
- **Official gift cards** are US-only, for US-based ChatGPT accounts billed in USD, redeemed at [chatgpt.com/redeem](https://chatgpt.com/redeem), and cannot pay App Store or Google Play subscriptions. See [Buying and redeeming OpenAI gift cards](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001491-buying-and-redeeming-openai-gift-cards).

### Regional and payment limits

OpenAI’s [ChatGPT Supported Countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-supported-countries) list is the official availability list. As of this verification, it **does not include mainland China, Hong Kong, or Macau**. **Taiwan is listed.** The article says that accessing or offering access outside the listed places “may result in your account being blocked or suspended.”

[ChatGPT and API services in unsupported countries and territories](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9131992-chatgpt-and-api-services-in-unsupported-countries-and-territories) also states that travel into an unsupported region can break access even for an account created in a supported region, and that **payment methods from unsupported countries will result in you being blocked from using the services**.

This repo does not provide instructions for bypassing regional restrictions.

## Claude (Anthropic)

Official page: [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing). Personal plans: **Free, Pro, Max, Ultra**. Prices exclude tax and may change.

| Plan | Official price (USD) | Official usage relationship | Best for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Limited every 5 hours and every week | Light trials | [Pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) |
| Pro | **$20**/month, or **$200** billed annually (shown as **$17**/month) | More than Free; at least 5× Free per 5-hour session | Everyday work, Claude Code, Projects, more models | Same page; [What is the Pro plan?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan) |
| Max | **$100**/month (monthly only) | **5× Pro** every 5 hours and every week | All-day work | [Pricing](https://claude.com/pricing); [What is the Max plan?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11049741-what-is-the-max-plan) |
| Ultra | **$200**/month (monthly only) | **10× Pro** every week; **no 5-hour limit** for verified accounts | Long-running work and heavy Claude Code | [Pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) |

Official details verified 2026-11-15:

- Web, desktop, mobile, and Claude Code share one usage pool. There is no fixed message count.
- Pro and above include Claude Code, task hand-off / scheduling, more models, Claude in Chrome, and Microsoft 365 integrations.
- Max and Ultra include priority access at busy times and monthly Claude Platform API credits: **$100** on Max, **$200** on Ultra.
- Fable is pay-with-credits on Pro; Max and Ultra can use it for up to half of the weekly limit.
- After a limit: wait for the reset, upgrade, or (on paid plans) enable usage credits at standard API rates.

Some Chinese-language listings still say “Max 5x / Max 20x.” On 2026-11-15 the official consumer names are **Pro, Max (5×), and Ultra ($200, 10× weekly)**. Match the live official names, not informal labels.

### Regional policy

Use the [Supported Regions Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/supported-regions) (effective **2026-11-12**). Any country or region not listed is unsupported. The policy is broader than physical location: it also covers people who are physically in an unsupported region (including staff or visitors of otherwise supported organizations), entities incorporated or headquartered in an unsupported region and their personnel even if those people are in a supported region, and entities majority-owned or controlled from unsupported regions.

As of this verification, the list **does not include mainland China, Hong Kong, or Macau**. **Taiwan is listed.** This legal page is newer than the help-center article “Where can I access Claude?”; if they differ, follow the policy’s effective text.

## How to choose

These are reading notes from official positioning, not benchmark scores.

- **Occasional questions:** any free tier.
- **Daily chat, writing, light image use:** cheaper entry plans such as ChatGPT Go (US $8) or Google AI Plus ($4.99).
- **Coding, research, stronger reasoning:** ChatGPT Plus ($20) or Claude Pro ($20, or $200/year). Official docs place Claude Code and similar tools on Pro and above.
- **All-day use that hits ordinary paid limits:** Claude Max (5× Pro, $100) or ChatGPT Pro 100 / 200; for still longer Claude sessions, Ultra ($200, 10× weekly). ChatGPT Ultrafast is on Pro 500 only.
- **Grok + Cursor + X in one bill:** The X Subscription. Official FAQ suggests Plus for light AI, Premium for many new users, Super for daily agents, Ultra for power users.
- **Already deep in Gmail, Docs, and Drive:** Google AI Pro / Ultra also bundle storage and some YouTube / Home / Health benefits.

## Subscribing from China

This section lists public options and documented risks. **It does not provide steps for bypassing regional or payment restrictions.**

### 1. Official checkout

Subscribe on the official site or app with a supported payment method (often a major credit card, or a supported app-store account) to **your own account**. This is the most direct path and the one that best matches each vendor’s terms. It usually requires both a supported region and a supported payment method. OpenAI states that payment methods from unsupported countries will get you blocked.

### 2. Official gift cards (mainly OpenAI today)

OpenAI’s official ChatGPT gift cards are narrowly scoped: buy and redeem only in the United States, only on US USD-billed accounts, on the web, and not for App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Sending a US card abroad does not make it eligible there. As of this verification, this guide has not found an equivalent official gift-card program with the same documented scope from Anthropic, xAI, or Google. Do not treat a third-party “redeem code” as an official gift card.

### 3. Third-party top-up / code redemption

“Top-up” or “direct top-up” usually means a third party helps activate an official subscription **on your own account** when you cannot pay officially. A redeem code is the self-service variant of the same idea. This is different from a **shared or rented account**, which typically violates terms of use and is not private. This repo **does not recommend** shared accounts.

If you consider a third-party channel, check at least these six points (adapted from the [Chinese top-up guide](https://www.aizc.club/guide/ai-daichong)):

1. The plan is on your own account and visible in official billing or subscription settings.
2. Account requirements are published before purchase (existing subs, store billing, trial or unusual payment history, any required official verification).
3. Warranty and refund rules are public before you pay.
4. A price far below the official list price is a warning sign.
5. You get an order ID and a stable support channel.
6. Do not use a shared account to save money.

## Account-safety notes

- Never give anyone your password. A legitimate own-account top-up should not need it.
- OpenAI’s help center says sharing credentials, reselling access, and automatically extracting data are prohibited and can lead to restrictions. See [About ChatGPT Pro tiers](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers).
- Avoid shared or rented accounts.
- Treat official gift-card PINs as secrets. A redeemed PIN cannot be returned to the card.
- If you already subscribe through Apple or Google Play, cancel there and wait for the term to end before switching paths, to avoid double charges or a failed activation.
- Be skeptical of prices far below the official list, or of promises that an account will “never be banned.”
- OpenAI’s usage-policy update effective **2026-11-20** is real, but the official text does not announce a “whitelist,” and it does not write a China-wide ban as a new rule for that date. The clearly new language is mainly that model-access intermediaries need prior approval. Background: [this site’s fact-check](https://www.aizc.club/blog/openai-nov-20-usage-policies-whitelist-claim).
